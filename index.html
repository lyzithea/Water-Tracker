How much water do I need?💧

It is recommended for adults to drink at least 1.5 liters of water per day. However, the actual amount varies greatly depending on your lifestyle and diet. For instance, athletes should aim for 2 to 3 liters - especially when consuming creatine, as it binds water in the muscles.

A high-fiber diet also requires a higher fluid intake. A simple and reliable indicator of the optimal intake is the color of your urine: if it is pale yellow to almost clear, you are drinking just the right amount.
